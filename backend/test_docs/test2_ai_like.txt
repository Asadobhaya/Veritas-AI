The process of photosynthesis is fundamental to life on Earth. Through this mechanism, plants convert sunlight into chemical energy. The process occurs primarily in the chloroplasts of plant cells. Light energy is absorbed by chlorophyll and other pigments. Water molecules are split to release oxygen. Carbon dioxide is fixed into organic molecules. The Calvin cycle produces glucose from carbon dioxide. This glucose serves as the primary energy source for the plant. The overall equation can be summarized simply. Six molecules of carbon dioxide combine with six molecules of water. Light energy drives the reaction forward efficiently. The products are one molecule of glucose and six molecules of oxygen.
